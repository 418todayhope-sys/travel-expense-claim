# Travel expense claim — Task and submission

## Scenario

Alderbridge Consulting's operations lead needs a dependable, traceable way to handle travel expenses as claims, decisions and supporting information change. Interview the lead to understand the work, then build a reusable Agent Skill that supports the complete process. The company and records are fictional.

## Your task

Create your own repository from the public GitHub template. It supplies an entry README and the required snapshot schema; you build the Skill, implementation, checks and outputs. Use Git, an AI coding agent, the program's supported session capture, and browser or supported API/export access. Choose your implementation language and document any additional setup. No real employee identity, bank account or paid data source is needed.Implement the assigned work across all supplied records and arrival batches: travel permission and claim handling, monetary review, human decisions, reimbursement reconciliation and closure, corrections, and independent trip or permit cancellations, including processes without a claim. Build and demonstrate a connected ordinary path, then cover the material exceptions and later changes found in the sources. Individual cases may remain unresolved when the facts require it; report the affected work, reason, responsible person and evidence needed to resume. Missing company rules or inaccessible required sources must be raised with the lead or facilitator rather than guessed.

Also implement a local work queue or request drafts for missing evidence and returned work. Choose its organization and partial-response handling. Keep individual work traceable to its subject, revision, source, missing fact or return reason, responsible person and next action. Show significant queue updates with their run/batch references in or through the report. Apply your chosen organization throughout the applicable records; the supplied later batches are fixed inputs, not events caused by your drafts.


Obtain the five native source locations through the interview. Inputs include searchable PDFs, a Google Sheets workbook and a Notion page; business data is not bundled in the starter. Every new run must attempt fresh reads of all five locations through a browser or supported API/export route. Retain the complete relevant content actually used, including its original wording, identities, precision and history, and bind it in `sources.json`. Preserve the supplied case clock and arrival boundaries. Record inaccessible, partial or incompatible content and its affected scope explicitly. A URL, successful response or hash alone is not evidence of complete access. Do not alter sources or silently substitute bundled inputs, earlier downloads or an AI paraphrase. A labelled offline replay may reuse an earlier run's retained inputs without claiming fresh access.

People retain business approval, exception and payment authority. The Skill prepares local work, validates and consumes authorized scenario decisions, and records their effects; it must not fabricate a reply, make professional approval recommendations, send real requests, book travel or move money..

In your captured coding conversation, make and explain four source-grounded choices: how incomplete claims affect independent work; which work to revalidate after material corrections; the financial-completion conditions and delivery scope you accept after inspecting an actual result; and the organization and partial-response constraints for repair work. Adopt these constraints before they guide the work, connect them to actual source/subject and code/run references, and show their effect in the implementation and results. You may adopt an agent suggestion with your own source-grounded reason and constraint. Record an owned unresolved step where the evidence does not support completion, and reconsider affected choices when later evidence arrives. Concise statements in your normal conversation are enough; no separate decision register or fabricated mistake is required.


Verify the ordinary path and every material branch in the supplied records, including later corrections, cancellations and replay. Check quantities, identities, source bindings and state transitions as well as status labels. Additional isolated test inputs are allowed but do not replace the primary run. Document unrun checks and implementation limitations honestly; a case-count or time limit does not remove assigned work.
